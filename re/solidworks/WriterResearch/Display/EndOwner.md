# Ordinary blind boss end specification after the distance display

The scoped generation-19000 writer constructs the parent's 101-byte continuation from the completed distance display through its owned sketch-plane start wrapper. It records the following 20 bytes as signed native properties. Those properties remain outside construction. `moExtrusion_c` has a separate 12-byte return followed by the four-byte resolved-list footer. [Typed codec](EndOwner.py), [display boundary](Boundaries.md)

Both analyses agree on the field owners and ordering. [Earlier owner map](../../records/Serialize.md)

All offsets below begin immediately after the 1,807-byte `moDisplayDistanceDim_c` record. The active blind length belongs to the preceding display. The enclosing direction is read from the `moEndSpec_c` member `+0x8c`, stored as a signed long at `0x4bb0f62c` before the display. It agrees with the independently measured operation in all eight saves, including the negative control. Both end conditions are zero, the end is single-direction, and the depth equals the positive driving parameter in every file. ## Remaining properties

`moEndSpec_c` writes five signed longs after the start wrapper at `0x4bb0f89a`, `0x4bb0f8a9`, `0x4bb0f8b8`, `0x4bb0f8c7` and `0x4bb0f900`. They are `CapEnd[0]`, `CapEnd[1]`, `DelInitFace`, `KnitRes` and `CreateSolidFrmCappedMidPlaneSurfExt`. The last word uses store generation gate `0x3b01`. The native getters return the raw members, and the modern loader reads directly into the members. The exact raw tuples vary across the pinned controls. [Raw signed field controls](Boundaries.md#complete-display-and-enclosing-return)

The native constructor at `0x4bad28b0` explicitly clears these five fields. It clears `+0x158` at `0x4bad2983`, `+0x148/+0x14c` at `0x4bad2a70`, and `+0x150/+0x154` at `0x4bad2a77`. This is positive initialization evidence for this constructor and build. The constructor word values do not explain the later saved values by themselves. Establishing which later writes or consumers apply to the plain blind solid, particularly why `CapEnd[1]` changes only in the negative control, is the next required boundary before emitting the five properties as a construction rule.
