# Ordinary blind boss base display return

The 370-byte `moDisplayDim_c` return occupies +14–384 after the favorite tag in the generation-19000 P03 distance display. The research encoders construct three bounded spans totaling 366 bytes. They leave the signed `SelLeader` field at +344–348 outside generation. All offsets below are relative to the favorite class-reference tag. [Favorite and subclass boundaries](DisplayFavorite.md)

The source uses SOLIDWORKS 34.4.1.1011. The `sldmodu.dll` SHA-256 is `2201734ebe0106efde5a93e306067b4b5e2fdb0ba70bf38ce9a59242b283cb99`. The `sldmfcu.dll` SHA-256 is `bea7d36e54929371aa9d6db9faa3210b8661a14038eb1f0fcac714c6d110e9a6`. The archive count serializer is in `swccu.dll` SHA-256 `7254b30f4eb77fd5b49fdbb0a7ab499ba3bf1d39a3f8f18d72090c2568967b49`. The preferred virtual addresses below refer to those builds.

## Mask construction

The binary display serializer writes `Mask2` as an unsigned long at `0x4c82b533`. The field at `+0xb60` starts at `0xfc` from `0x4c887f83`. The feature constructor sets bits 0 and 17 at `0x4c86d8e6–0x4c86d8f7`. It sets bits 14, 21, 22, 23 and 29–31 at `0x4c86d928–0x4c86d940`, clears bit 25 at `0x4c86d955` and stores at `0x4c86d959`. The constructor obtains application toggle `0x387` at `0x4c86d906–0x4c86d90e` and sets or clears bit 8 at `0x4c86d91a–0x4c86d920`. The exported `moDisplayDim_c::isDesignIntent` at `0x4b28d720` reads that bit. The research caller supplies the design-intent preference.

The serializer writes `Mask3` from `+0xb64` at `0x4c82bc5f`. Initialization at `0x4c887f8d` sets `0x1870`. The ordinary feature constructor sets bits 0, 2, 9, 14, 16–19 and clears bits 3 and 8 at `0x4c86d944–0x4c86da37`. Three named settings supply the remaining bits.

| Bit | Source setting | Native link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 | Apply inward rounding to tolerance | Document dimension definition member `+0x78`, getter `getApplyInwardRngToTol` at `0x3c767f20`. Copied at `0x4c888549–0x4c888559`. |
| 13 | Remove zero | Document dimension definition member `+0x9c`, getter `getIsRemoveZero` at `0x3c763df0`. Copied at `0x4c888561–0x4c888574`. |

The eight saved masks are `0xe0e241fd` and `0x000f4a75`. The explicit settings `design_intent=True`, `inward_tolerance_rounding=False`, `remove_zero=False` and `uppercase_dimension=False` reproduce both words through the native-source bit rules. The archived measurement receipt records model geometry and dimensions. The fixture supplies the four settings explicitly because the measurement receipt omits them. ## Inactive oracle controls

The production reader's typed `ReadDisplayTail` entry was followed for each exact P01 and P03 archive pinned by its save receipt and measurement receipt. Each favorite payload returned at +436 from the same entry. The two signed words at +338 and +342 were followed by three zero shifted-point coordinates and two clear flags for all 36 observations.

| Owner | Pinned records | `SetPoint` | Observed `SelLeader` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| P03 rectangle sketch displays | 16 | 0 | 0 in 6, 3407917 in 6, 6029357 in 4 |
| P03 blind boss depth display | 8 | 0 | 6029357 in all 8 |

The same inactive saved layout therefore occurs with several raw selector values. The ordinary sketch displays establish a saved precedent for zero. The field-specific blind boss experiment uses the exact base selector position in the P03 baseline instead of extrapolating sketch authorship to a feature dimension. The scratch experiment starts from P03 `baseline-a` SHA-256 `db1390476325f5817e6f80fb4067a3c6df5cc063315604c133ccbeaafdeac357`. The repacked control SHA-256 is `b93dc8adec77a30eeaa68b8664989abf9b1524ff072c9d0829226337b6d338bf`. It preserves every expanded stream byte. The candidate SHA-256 is `8c5f7b7330a2d3675662f5aaa3f2f03d548daa923e442b04f44ba745549a67dd`. Its only expanded stream difference from the repacked control is the signed zero field at offsets `[14806, 14810)` of `Contents/Config-0-ResolvedFeatures`. The same compact favorite occurrence puts those offsets at +344–348. Each input is a private scratch copy keyed by the experiment manifest SHA-256 `c90bd368780308d7beee40e1a940d5905efa54c49fb9008d1db3219d67f01d15`.

Licensed SOLIDWORKS 34.4.1.1011 opened the original, repacked control and zero-selector candidate read-only with zero errors and warnings. Each retained the `RectangleSketch` profile, `BlindBoss` extrusion, driving `depth_mm` display and typed blind depth of 10 mm. Before and after rebuild, volume was 8,000 mm³ and centroid was `[20, 10, 5]` mm. The retained candidate measurement SHA-256 is `284ba8cb0e07964063e702a4d2b2da48e5a99f5027d218bde39ad1ea9339805c`. The held candidate process exited with code zero. The watchdog-owned run confirmed the in-process healthy control before the candidate and a separate fresh healthy control afterward, each with zero open diagnostics and volume 881,814.3482038013 mm³ before and after rebuild. Both controls are pinned to the same input digest `d1bbca099ccac1330024619390df3c6b76320a6a6c281a5009578b2fb63e7408`.

The trailing control returned a nonzero process exit after measuring, rebuilding and closing its document. Its native process exit code was not retained. The watchdog recorded a failed campaign and an empty private job, with receipt SHA-256 `2ebb0874b7760741a5ead4258520da9725b4728a11116fda702e9741bf29a537`. An earlier attempt reproduced the candidate measurement but timed out during its in-session trailing control. Treat the completed measurements as a field-isolation oracle. They support zero as a deliberate emitted value for the ordinary generation-19000 blind boss route with `SetPoint` clear and the shifted point at its default origin. They leave active foreshortening, dimension edit and save/reopen behavior, and full independent writer acceptance open.
