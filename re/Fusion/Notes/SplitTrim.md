`fusion-trim-crash-investigation.json` records the interrupted journals, process-loss observations and sanitized excerpts from two local Autodesk CER crash stacks. The hold was checked offline without touching Fusion. No additional live reproduction, automatic restart or crash-report submission was performed during the investigation. At that stage, an object-lifetime repair and single-case validation were pending explicit authorization. The subsequently authorized repair and native validation are recorded below; the original failed runs and crash report remain unchanged historical evidence.

The user explicitly authorized fixing the capture crashes and continuing with Fusion. `GenerateSurfaceTrimToolContrasts.py` now puts native capture work in an inner frame that returns serialized data only. In the rolled-before-feature state, it releases the TrimFeature, input, cell collection, last cell and bounding-box aliases before restoring the timeline. That destroys the native hidden Trim input while its document and transaction context still exist. After the frame returns, garbage collection runs with the document open. A separate short frame reacquires only the design/root, recomputes and exports; that frame is released and collected before the outer loop closes the owned document. Failed or uncertain transactions retain explicit owned native handles on the stable `adsk.fusion` module and halt before export, close or another case. Exception objects and traceback frames do not escape into the saved data. No unconditional `applyCellChanges` call is used as cleanup.
