## SDK coverage

Kit also projects ordered historical whole-body inputs for qualified local
Combine, Scale, Mirror, and Shell features. It matches the proxy and its
reciprocal support list, then checks each descriptor against the historical
body UUID, source Extrude, and shared component scope. Combine keeps the
authored tool order, separate target role, operation, and keep-tool setting.
A consumed tool remains a historical selection even when it has left the
saved body inventory. These selections use a `historical_body` query and an
empty topology path.

The public reader recovered 44 inputs from 30 tracked features, including
swapped Combine roles, a two-body Shell whose order differs from its body
inventory, and Mirror's repeated collection order. Face and occurrence
contexts and other historical body creators remain outside this selection
projection.

Qualified terminal Scale features also reconstruct their final prism solids
from the source sketches, Extrude distances, and ordered Scale controls.
The SDK retains uniform or per-axis parameter links and a separately verified
component origin, sketch point, or ByPoint construction centre. Every
transformed prism must match the mapped saved ASM body's identity, incidence,
vertices, bounded edges, and face planes before Kit publishes the final set.
Six tracked captures passed, including expansion, contraction, nonuniform
scaling, and a two-body Scale around a displaced point. B-rep vertex centres
remain open.

These six captures also export as live FreeCAD Sketcher, Extrude, and Scale
features. The Parameters sheet drives source depths and uniform or per-axis
factors. A linked native point follows the authored sketch point or ByPoint
support. Each result retains a distinct body tip and an independently
generated first-open solid cache. Kit reconciles FreeCAD 1.1.3 saves by
checking the original links, solved sheet values, sketch caches, and both the
source and result solids. It accepts uniform and per-axis factor edits,
direct and ByPoint centre edits, profile-corner edits, and signed depth edits
for these line-prism histories. It preserves the source identities and
authored body order. The independent readback checks covered 23 native saves
and 31 result solids, including a reversed two-body control, and rejected
27 saves or mutations with stale geometry or conflicting controls. FreeCAD
reopened and rebuilt all 16 edited SDK re-exports. Five further native edits
passed a second save, reopen, rebuild, and SDK reconciliation cycle.
