## SDK coverage

The document reader also projects sketch-local points, lines, circles,
and supported circular arcs with a support frame derived from each
sketch's verified component transform. The SDK matched all 1,005 tracked
sketch placements and 6,589 projected points, lines, and circles against
the study. All 38 planar arcs matched native trim geometry. A projected
arc outside local XY remains unsupported. The 13 newer Revolve captures
project their 13 sketches and 117 primitives.

Kit also reads core geometric constraints and dimensional constraints from
the sketch's native relation lists. It checks the typed operand records,
their sketch ownership, and the repeated argument IDs. Supported dimensions
retain the original parameter and distinguish driving dimensions from
reference measurements. Aligned, horizontal, vertical, radius, diameter,
and positive directed angular controls are supported. The 712-archive
corpus contains 14 qualified dimensions and 12 recognized variants that
still produce diagnostics.

The FreeCAD writer links Fusion's explicit point identities to their line
and arc endpoints or circular centers. These links keep the curves attached
when the solver moves a point. Driving dimensions use the spreadsheet
quantity in native solver units, and reference dimensions measure the
solved geometry. Collinearity is retained in the neutral sketch and carried
in FreeCAD. Recognized unresolved source relations contribute to the mixed
sketch transfer report. Additional curve layouts, sketch tools, and native
support links remain open.

FreeCAD 1.1.3 checked 25 native Sketcher cases from 13 installed SDK exports
against the saved Fusion measurements. Eleven dimensional edits survived
native save and reopen. A driving radius changed from 12.5 to 18 mm, a
reference radius followed a separate geometry edit, and moving a coincident
endpoint moved both lines.

Newly exported standalone sketches with supported point, line, and circle
geometry can also be read back after a FreeCAD save. Kit checks the original
constraint and expression bindings, the worksheet values, the solved sketch
geometry, and its saved shape. Driving dimensions retain their literal edits,
and reference dimensions take their value from the solved geometry. The
verified saves include radius, diameter, angle, a reference radius, and
joined endpoints moved by dragging. The radius control passed a second
native edit after re-export, from 18 to 21.25 mm. Older unbound archives are
read as source snapshots. This standalone binding requires a resolved sketch
graph without downstream modeling features.

For Extrude and Revolve, the document reader follows typed profile
descriptors to their owning feature, sketch, and reciprocal loop record.
It preserves the native member order and direction flags separately from
the neutral traversal order. Single outer loops formed by a whole circle,
a semicircle with its diameter, or a closed convex line ring become linked
feature inputs. The SDK matched 501 Extrude and 26 Revolve profiles in
the tracked corpus, plus all 13 newer Revolve captures. Trimmed, concave, and multi-loop selections remain
outside this projection.

### Saved study findings

Study scripts, reports and capture paths in this section are relative to [the Study directory](../Study/README.md).

`sketch-tool-decoding.json` compares ten offset/pattern cases, including both offset signs, closed rectangle/circle offsets, full/partial/symmetric circular patterns and spacing/extent rectangular patterns. Relationships use explicit entity lists and numeric bindings; stored curve positions are independently compared with the API. The first export retains three documented-null-direction API errors; a focused retry creates those three rectangular cases using numeric property setters. The decoder reports these relationships under sketch `constraints`, with recognized-but-unsupported layouts listed in `sketch_decoding.tool_constraints.unresolved`. Offset fixed/construction state, secondary tag semantics, topology-match flags and instance suppression remain partial.

`sketch-pattern-detail-decoding.json` adds 30 reciprocal circle/center-point/helper chains across the six existing single-circle patterns. Native controller dependency order is retained separately from stored seed/created member order. Each center point names its helper twice, and that typed helper links back to the same circle and point. Seventy-eight geometry comparisons and three malformed-link checks support this addition. The main reader exposes these associations under `sketch_pattern_details`; no lattice positions, suppression state, multi-entity grouping or selected-direction semantics are inferred.

`sketch-pattern-mode-decoding.json` expands the main sketch-tool reader using eight new captures: two-circle and mixed line/circle seeds, a single line, explicit oblique rectangular direction lines, and circular/rectangular suppression. Counted suppression ordinals and direction references make the controller layout variable-length. Explicit seed-prefix and active instance-major member groups retain source associations without geometry matching. All 130 API comparisons, six earlier pattern cases and five malformed checks pass; four existing offsets remain readable. Symmetric suppression is rejected until its mapping is captured, and the shared dependency reader retains its 16-argument limit. Optional legacy center-helper details are skipped with a reason for expanded shapes; the full supported constraint remains in the sketch's `constraints` list.

`sketch-symmetric-index-decoding.json` adds eight successful exports across `sketchsymmetricindices-2ba1d336c9cf`, `sketchsymmetricindices-bde2c81eaa4a` and `sketchsymmetricindices-daa09c9428a1`. The rule derives a seed position inside the native lattice, then maps a suppressed native index `r` to API ordinal `r+1` below the seed or `r` above it; the seed cannot be suppressed.
