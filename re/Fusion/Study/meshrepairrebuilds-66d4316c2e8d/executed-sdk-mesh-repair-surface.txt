class MeshRepairTypes(object):
    r"""
    ** This enum is a preview feature and may change in future releases. Do not use it in distributed programs. **

    Specify the main repair type for the mesh. If RebuildMeshRepairType is chosen, other parameters can be set.

    Attributes:
        CloseHolesMeshRepairType: Simplest and fastest type of repair, only close holes and fix flipped triangles are used to fix the part.
