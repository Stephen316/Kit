# Fusion sketch basis and principal datum

In the saved P01 and P03 YZ archives, the sketch and selected datum share the origin and +X normal. Their in-plane axes differ. Preserve the sketch transform for its local geometry and resolve the datum identity through the feature's saved selection and reciprocal component origin records. A coincident frame alone does not establish the attachment. [Saved support projection](../../../../Changes/Fusion/Adapter/FusionPrincipalSketchSupport.md)
