# Timeline presence does not establish active model history

A plane-only document can keep a cached frame and a typed `BoundDatum` step while
global or configuration suppression disables that entire timeline. There may be
no body or sketch to trigger their ownership guards. A nonempty tuple therefore
does not establish active generating history.

The CLI rejects nonempty timelines with no active features after suppression and
dependency filtering. Standalone support planes also require SDK geometry
qualification even with an active datum reference. Preserve these documents and
use `kit decode` or deterministic research inspection while their reconstruction
contract is unsupported.

See [the active-history guard](../../../Changes/Commands/ActiveModelHistory.md).
