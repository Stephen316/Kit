# SOLIDWORKS configuration model presentation

The [research codec](../../../../../re/solidworks/WriterResearch/ConfigModelView.py) constructs the local model unit component, the optional curvature appearance and the reference-plane display settings after the generation-19000 units table. The component aliases its enclosing model. Curvature presence, settings and creation time are independent inputs. [Native field evidence and replay](../../../../../re/solidworks/WriterResearch/ConfigModelView.md)

Read-only comparison checked the typed span and archive map for all six P01 and eight P03 saves. Hosted byte comparison is wired and pending. The supported span ends at the bounding-box selector and does not establish native application acceptance.
