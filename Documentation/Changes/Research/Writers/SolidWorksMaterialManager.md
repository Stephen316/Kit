# Encode the empty SOLIDWORKS material manager

The [A12 research codec](../../../../re/solidworks/WriterResearch/MaterialManager.py) emits a caller-supplied appearance allocation counter followed by the zero 16-bit count of an empty material object list. The reader starts at the caller's counter offset and returns the unsigned counter and the exact next offset. It refuses truncated fields and any nonzero list count, including the marker for a larger count. The enclosing document owns the manager presence field and the following `CString`.

In installed SOLIDWORKS 34.4.1.1011, `sldmodu.dll` SHA-256 `2201734ebe0106efde5a93e306067b4b5e2fdb0ba70bf38ce9a59242b283cb99` exports `?getNewApperanceID@PWMaterialManager_c@@QEAA_KXZ`, which MSVC undecorates as returning `unsigned __int64`. The method at `0x4c1fd340` reads and increments the QWORD at member offset `+8`. The two store calls at `0x4c204871` and `0x4c20487d` submit the low and high words to `su_CArchive::operator<<(long)`. Its binary branch in `swccu.dll` writes each DWORD directly at `0x31a4486b` and advances four bytes. The combined little-endian unsigned scalar therefore preserves both signed-word byte encodings. [Native serializer evidence](../../../../re/solidworks/WriterResearch/DefinitionJournal.md#appearance-id-counter)

All 25 receipt-pinned P01, P03 and P04 captures place this ten-byte fragment at notebook end `+13` and resume the document's string at `+23`. Their list counts are zero. Populated material objects and whole-document generation remain open. The codec stays in research and has static verification only.
