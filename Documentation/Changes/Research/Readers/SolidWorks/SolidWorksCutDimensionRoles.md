# Saved SOLIDWORKS cut sketch constraints

The P04 CutSketch reads its four saved axis relations against the configuration line members. The dimension constraints retain their previous IDs and use the saved point order. Width and height drive the actual endpoints of their perimeter lines. The offset dimensions are derived through the saved centering equations and use the authored corner and the projected Origin point. The face frame and its historical cap selection stay in place.

The Origin reference resolves to the earlier owned feature and its saved point handle before the point can appear in the cut sketch. Constraint provenance identifies the exact configuration records. Parameter provenance retains the canonical length scalar and its equation source where present. The perimeter line IDs still refer to the configuration members, independently of the resolved region chain IDs.

Replaying all eleven pinned P04 files matched 44 native axis targets and 44 ordered dimension operands against the licensed reopen. The 22 size dimensions were driving and the 22 offset dimensions were driven. The YZ case swaps the local offset axes and retains a negative 6 mm horizontal bottom measurement with a positive 6 mm cached scalar. The inch display text and existing parameter values, IDs and expressions stay intact. A mismatched Origin withholds the entire constraint projection.
