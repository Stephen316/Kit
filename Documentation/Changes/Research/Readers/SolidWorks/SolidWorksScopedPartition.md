# SOLIDWORKS two-body active partition

The bounded [partition reader](../../../../../src/convert/adapters/solidworks/Core/Partition/ScopedPartition.py) takes one decompressed Parasolid partition payload and two stable design body IDs keyed by the recent atom IDs established by typed feature history. It returns the saved B-rep with one linked carrier per solid region and per-body bounds, topology counts, volume, and geometric centroid.

The native shell and region records must refer to each other and to the same body owner. Each shell has a complete face chain with reciprocal references. Its exact face IDs identify one decoded solid region, and the named `ATOM_ID_2001` attribute assigns the body's recent atom. The reader requires a distinct atom for each owner and an exact match with the supplied atom keys. Each decoded topology record belongs to exactly one region. The reader measures each closed planar region from its own oriented loops.

The scope is two linear, planar solid regions under partition schema `SCH_3701250_37102_13006`. Body labels, region order, and geometry play no part in the identity join.

The [projection helper](../../../../../src/convert/adapters/solidworks/Core/Partition/ScopePartProjection.py) finds the unique Parasolid payload in the selected configuration's active partition and checks its SHA-256. It supplies stable design IDs from the original Boss body IDs. The primary explicit list or the later all-bodies list binds each affected body to its recent atom and the Cut as its final feature. An excluded body keeps its Boss atom and final feature. The projection retains the partition wrapper and each affected body's resolved record as separate provenance spans. It returns both design bodies and their linked B-rep together. The [public adapter](SolidWorksScopedNeutral.md) requires this result before publishing scoped history.

## Verification

Read-only replay verified the three retained archive hashes against the [licensed report](../../Workers/SolidWorks/ThroughAllScopeNativeEvidence.md). Both body bounds, centroids and topology counts agreed with the fresh-process measurements. Body 101 measured 7,200 mm³ in all three saves and linked to final feature 47. Body 102 measured 600 mm³ with recent atom 104 and final feature 47 in All bodies and Explicit A+B. In Explicit A, body 102 retained its Boss atom 102 and final feature 40 and measured 1,000 mm³. All three linked B-rep models passed validation with their two design IDs. The ordinary P04 through-all capture still returned its existing linked single body at 7,200 mm³ through the public adapter.
