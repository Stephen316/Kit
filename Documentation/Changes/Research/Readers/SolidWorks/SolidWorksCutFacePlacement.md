# SOLIDWORKS cut profile and historical face placement

The generation-19000 P04 CutSketch projection fills the neutral profile from four saved configuration line members. It follows their point slots into one directed loop and checks each line against its saved horizontal or vertical relation. The profile keeps its sketch, feature and parameter IDs. Line IDs and provenance come from the configuration geometry. The resolved profile chain and its region edge IDs remain separate.

The face projection requires that typed perimeter and a unique earlier boss whose saved positive cap owns the attachment. The frame uses the actual face origin and corroborates the saved model-to-sketch pose. The neutral plane and sketch feature refer to a historical face selection with the file, feature, time, cap occurrence and ordered boundary references. Its provenance identifies the support record, cap record and all four edge records in the resolved stream. The sketch feature depends on the boss through that saved support.

The previously empty public cut profiles supplied the input to a replay of the two pure projections. All eleven pinned P04 captures matched the independently reopened line endpoints and support frames. The baseline cap carries edges `4, 1, 2, 3` and the YZ cap carries `4, 3, 2, 1`. The depth edit moves the origin 12 mm along the normal. A missing perimeter or wrong boss leaves the input document intact.
