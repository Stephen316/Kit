# Compare SOLIDWORKS discovery extrusion directions

The SOLIDWORKS discovery census follows each P03 and P04 extrusion's neutral sketch identifier to its named profile. It compares the signed direction in the typed extrusion with the independently reopened sketch pose and the SOLIDWORKS reversal flag. A one-sided boss normally follows the sketch normal. A one-sided cut normally goes against it. The saved reversal flag changes that default. [SOLIDWORKS FeatureCut4 remarks](https://help.solidworks.com/2024/english/api/sldworksapi/solidworks.interop.sldworks~solidworks.interop.sldworks.ifeaturemanager~featurecut4.html)

Run `python re/automation/SolidworksCensus.py --output /private/SolidworksDiscoveryCensus.json` and inspect the `neutral.operation.<name>.profile` and `neutral.operation.<name>.effective_direction_world` comparisons. Expected profiles come from the fixed discovery recipes. The signed vectors come from the licensed reopen records. The census uses the angular tolerance in the paired specification. The roster includes the reverse boss, YZ bosses and cuts, ten blind cuts and a depthless through-all cut. Automatic and explicit body scope, additional extrusion modes and held-out behavior require separate evidence.
