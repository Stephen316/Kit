# Saved SOLIDWORKS P04 centering equations

The packaged reader walks the two-atom configuration, the loaded relations and both nested sketches, then finishes the presentation suffix. Its final archive counter must agree with the resolved feature header. The reader follows the resolved rectangle and cut profiles back to those exact configuration sketch allocations. Each equation checks the saved assignment, ordered dimension tokens, subtraction and divisor against the two text sides and the cached native lengths. The public reader retains the existing parameter identities and records the three exact dimension-slot reads on each centering assignment. This also preserves the measured metric lengths on the inch-display case.

All eleven P04 discovery variants pass the archived walk. The two target slots bind native CutSketch dimensions 39 and 40 to the pair of RectangleSketch dimensions 27 and 28 and the CutSketch sizes 36 and 37. The current scope covers the two centering dependencies. Face attachment and complete editable P04 reconstruction need additional native evidence.
