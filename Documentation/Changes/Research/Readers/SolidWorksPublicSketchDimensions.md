# Saved SOLIDWORKS rectangle dimension operands

Opening the ordinary generation-19000 P01 and P03 rectangle paths with `convert.open_document` binds each driving width and height to the ordered endpoints of its saved perimeter edge. The active `sgPntPntDist` records describe unsigned two-point distances. Each must agree with its stored length scalar, its existing public parameter and the geometry of its two endpoint handles. The sketch retains its prior parameter, constraint and entity IDs and preserves the configured display text. The two dimensional constraints are projected together once both bindings are proven.

Public reads of all fourteen pinned discovery captures matched 28 independently measured driving parameters and ordered point pairs. In both YZ controls the height runs from the origin corner along negative local X and remains a positive distance. The inch controls retain the displayed values 1.57 and 0.79 with exact stored lengths 0.04 m and 0.02 m. A mismatched scalar offset withheld both dimensional projections in each capture while the independently proven geometry remained available.
