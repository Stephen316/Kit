# Packaged P04 face support decoder

`ReadFaceSupport` in `convert.adapters.solidworks.Resolved.SupportPlane` follows a generation-19000 `moCompRefPlane_c` from an existing typed archive cursor in context zero. The nested plane and face components must identify the same local part. The decoder retains the support extent, face-plane and component object indices, point and normal, model-to-sketch transform, and the typed cap and four ordered boundary surface references. `RequireFaceCap` compares the file, feature, time and occurrence of the supporting cap and each ordered boundary against a reached boss chooser.

The continuous replay matches the support frame against the independently reopened CutSketch in all eleven pinned P04 discovery captures. The optional basis is identity when omitted. The YZ capture retains the explicit basis with world axes `(-Z, +Y, +X)`. The depth edit shifts the support origin to 12 mm along the saved normal.
